Nothing goes to waste

The peel left over from making gari is dried and milled into feed instead of being thrown away. It is high in starch and digestible, so it can replace a useful share of the imported maize in a feed mix.

Terms

Our standard trading terms

These apply to contracts in Ghana, across ECOWAS, and for export.

Lead times
Bulk 25kg sacks: 4–7 business days from order confirmation.
20–40 MT export containers: 10–14 business days upon manifest and freight scheduling.
Payment
Spot orders: 50% when you order, 50% when the truck is loaded and inspected.
Contract accounts: 14 to 30 days after delivery, once we have agreed credit terms.
If something is wrong
Tell us in writing within 72 hours of delivery, with a test from an independent lab. If the batch is over 10% moisture or over 10 mg/kg cyanide, we replace it.
Delivery terms
Collect from Wusuta (Ex-Works), we load at Tema Port (FOB), or we deliver to your warehouse in Accra, Kumasi or Takoradi (DAP).
