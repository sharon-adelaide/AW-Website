Standard Commercial Terms

Standard parameters applying to contracts across Ghana, ECOWAS and export routes.

Standard lead times
Bulk 25kg sacks: 4–7 business days from order confirmation.
20–40 MT export containers: 10–14 business days upon manifest and freight scheduling.
Payment frameworks
Spot orders: 50% deposit on order placement, 50% against inspection manifest at truck loading.
Contract accounts: Net 14 to Net 30 days post-delivery on approved credit history.
Quality claims & audits
Written notice within 72 hours of freight receipt, supported by an independent third-party lab assay. Batch replacement for lots breaching moisture (>10%) or cyanide (>10 mg/kg).
Incoterms & logistics
Ex-Works (Wusuta, Volta Region), Free-On-Board (FOB Tema Port), or Delivered At Place (DAP buyer warehouse — Accra, Kumasi or Takoradi).
